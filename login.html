Terms of Service

StudyFlow AI Institutional Placement Platform

1. Academic Integrity: By creating an account on StudyFlow AI, you affirm that all academic credentials, roll numbers, marks, and profile details provided are accurate and reflect your official collegiate record.

2. Role Authorization: Student accounts are open to enrolled campus students. Faculty Mentor and Training & Placement Officer accounts require valid institutional authorization passcodes.

3. Placement Prediction Usage: Predictive metrics generated by the machine learning engine are intended for academic guidance and targeted readiness preparation.

4. Local Data Security: All user records are persistently managed via SQLite database architecture with salted bcrypt password hashing and tokenized session controls.

🛡️

Privacy Policy

Confidentiality and student academic data protection

1. Data Confidentiality: Your academic scores, resume drafts, and skill metrics are only accessible to authorized institutional mentors and placement officers.

2. Password Protection: StudyFlow AI never stores plain-text passwords. All credentials are hashed using industry-standard bcrypt encryption.

3. SQLite Database Storage: Academic profile data is persisted in a local SQLite database (data/studyflow.db) with full relational integrity and foreign-key constraints.

4. Role Isolation: Server-side role enforcement guarantees that students, faculty, and administrators only access permitted views and endpoints.
